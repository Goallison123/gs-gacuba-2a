# GS Gacuba Brand Identity Guidelines

## 1. Logo Usage
- The primary emblem features the school crest with the official motto: *"Excellence in Education"*.
- Safe margin around the logo: At least equal to the height of the letter 'G' in the logo mark.
- Logo colorways: Full color on light backgrounds; inverse/white variant on deep navy surfaces.

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Authoritative yet Welcoming**: Speaking with dignity and respect as an established educational pillar.
- **Student-Centric**: Celebrating pupil achievements in both academics and sports/culture.
- **Forward-Looking**: Emphasizing digital literacy, science, and character development.
