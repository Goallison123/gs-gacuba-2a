# Information Architecture & Sitemap

## 1. Page Readiness & Release Status

The platform delivers five fully realized, production-ready pages alongside responsive modals and centralized command palette search:

- **Active & Production-Ready**:
  - `Home` (`/` or `home`) — Comprehensive landing page with dynamic hero section, institution statistics (800+ Students, NESA Accredited), academic streams highlight, value propositions, outstanding student achievements, and admission callout.
  - `About Us` (`/about`) — School history, mission, vision, foundation pillars, headmaster's welcoming address (HABUMUGISHA Ernest), and leadership board framed against the official vector contour mesh background artwork.
  - `Gallery` (`/gallery`) — Clean, balanced two-column hero layout with bold typography, quote card, coral subtitle, campus visual tour badge, category filtering (All, School Life, Events, Academics, Sports), 3-column media cards with interactive like/dislike feedback, and numeric pagination.
  - `News & Announcements` (`/news`) — Breaking news announcement ticker (*"Latest: Our student awarded a 1st position in National exams 2026"*), featured award story with interactive like & share counters and CTA overlays, category filter tabs, 3-card news grid with Lenovo laptop delivery coverage, and full pagination.
  - `Academics` (`/academics`) — Split diagonal hero banner with classroom photography (`/assets/c1786.png`), dual CTAs, interactive academic pathway segmented control switching between Advanced Level (MS2 and Arts & Humanities) and Ordinary Level (CBC Core Foundations and STEM & Digital Literacy), and golden-orange call-to-action banner.

- **Coming Soon (Phase 2 Roadmap)**:
  - `Admissions`, `Student Life`, `Facilities`, `Leadership`, `Careers`, `FAQ`, and `Contact` provide direct navigation back to the active pages and open the Admission / Inquiry modals.
